Company: Nimbus Cloud Storage Pvt Ltd

About Us:
Nimbus Cloud Storage was founded in 2019. We provide secure cloud backup
solutions for small businesses. Our headquarters is located in Kochi, Kerala.

Refund Policy:
Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase if they are
not satisfied with the service. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business
days and are credited back to the original payment method.

Support Hours:
Our customer support team is available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM IST.
Support can be reached via email at support@nimbuscloud.example or through
the live chat widget on our website.

Pricing Plans:
1. Starter Plan: 100 GB storage, priced at 499 INR per month.
2. Business Plan: 1 TB storage, priced at 1999 INR per month.
3. Enterprise Plan: Custom storage limits, contact sales for pricing.

Data Security:
All customer data is encrypted using AES-256 encryption both at rest and
in transit. We perform daily automated backups and store redundant copies
across three geographically separate data centers.
